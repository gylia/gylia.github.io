My work sits at the intersection of **embodied intelligence** and **multimodal learning**: teaching
robots to act in unstructured environments, and giving them senses beyond vision to do it with.

Two threads run through it. The first is **simulation and synthetic data** — building environments
and generative pipelines that produce supervision at a scale real-world collection cannot reach, then
closing the gap back to reality. The second is **evaluation** — benchmarks that measure what models
can actually do, rather than what they appear to do.

Alongside this, I have worked on **theoretical machine learning**: critical batch size and scaling
laws, statistical inference, and spectral methods for high-dimensional learning.
